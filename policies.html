Store Policies

Last updated: July 2026

Shipping Policy

All orders ship free within the United States with full tracking. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery typically takes 3–8 business days for items shipped from our US warehouses and 5–12 business days for items shipped from our overseas warehouses. Your tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order ships.

We currently ship to US addresses only. If your order hasn't arrived within 15 business days, contact us and we'll make it right — replacement or refund, your choice.

Returns & Refunds

Every order is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee, counted from the day your order is delivered. If you're not happy for any reason, email us and we'll issue a refund to your original payment method.

  • For most orders under $30, we issue the refund without requiring a return shipment.
  • For other orders we may ask you to mail the item back; we'll provide instructions by email.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of approval and appear on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Damaged or defective on arrival? Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll ship a free replacement or refund you in full — no return needed.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to fulfill your order: your name, shipping address, and email. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. Your shipping information is shared only with our fulfillment partners for the sole purpose of delivering your order. We never sell your personal information.

If you join our email list, we send occasional product updates and tips; every email includes a one-click unsubscribe. To request deletion of your data, email us and we'll confirm within 30 days.

Terms of Service

By placing an order you agree to provide accurate shipping information and to these policies. Prices are in US dollars. We reserve the right to cancel and fully refund any order we cannot fulfill. Product images are representative; minor variations in color may occur. Nothing on this site is veterinary advice — consult your vet for health concerns about your pet.
