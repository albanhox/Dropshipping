# Paid Ads Plan — When, How, and the Money Rules

**TL;DR: do not run ads yet.** Run them only after a video has proven it converts organically. Ads before that = paying to lose money. This doc is your trigger checklist and playbook for when you're ready.

## Why not now (the math)

Net profit ≈ $10/order (bundles ≈ $17). If your cost to get one sale ("CPA") is above that, every order loses money. Beginners on cold ads with untested creative routinely pay $15–40/sale. So day-one ads lose money on purpose.

## The trigger — flip ads ON only when ALL of these are true

- [ ] At least **one organic video** drove real link clicks → sales (you can see which one).
- [ ] You've banked **~$200 profit** you can afford to risk.
- [ ] Your **bundle ($44.99 kit)** is selling — bundles are what make ad math work.
- [ ] Refund rate is under ~5% (don't scale a leaky bucket).

Until every box is checked, keep doing organic + Marketplace.

## When ready: start with TikTok Spark Ads (not Facebook)

Spark Ads boost your *own organic video that already worked* — cheapest, most native, best for cat content.

- **Budget:** $10–20/day per ad, ONE proven video at a time.
- **Objective:** website clicks / conversions to your store URL.
- **Audience:** broad — TikTok's algorithm targets better than manual for impulse pet buys. US only, 18+, interests: cats/pets.
- **Never** run ads on a video that hasn't proven itself organically.

## The kill rules (obey these or you'll bleed money)

1. If an ad spends **2× your target CPA** (~$20) with **zero sales**, kill it. No "it'll turn around."
2. Judge results after **~$20–30 spend minimum** — don't kill at $3 or scale at $12; too little data.
3. Scale a winner **slowly** — +20–30% budget every 2–3 days, not double overnight (fast scaling breaks the algorithm's learning).
4. Track it simply: spend vs. sales-attributed-revenue per ad, daily. If revenue < spend after the data threshold, off.

## Facebook / Instagram — later

Add Meta ads only after TikTok is profitable, mainly for **retargeting** (people who visited but didn't buy). Cold Meta prospecting is harder and pricier for a new store.
