# Growth Plan — From $100/Week to $1,000/Month Profit

$1k/month net ≈ **100 orders/month at ~$10 net**, or **~55 orders at ~$18 net** once bundles raise your average order value. This doc is sequenced — do each stage only when the previous one's trigger fires.

## Stage 1 (weeks 2–4): Double down on what worked

**Trigger: you've made your first ~10 sales.**

- Kill the 2–3 products with no traction; go deeper on the 1–2 that sold. One winning product with 15 video angles beats 6 products with 2 angles each.
- **Buy a domain** (~$10/yr) and point it at the site — conversion rate rises with a real domain. Set up `support@yourdomain` (Zoho Mail free tier or Cloudflare Email Routing).
- Start collecting emails seriously: wire the footer form to a free Formspree/MailerLite account. The 10%-off code is a Stripe coupon (Dashboard → Coupons) applied to your Payment Links.
- Keep posting 2–3 videos/day. By week 4 you have 60–80 videos; expect 1–3 to have outperformed everything else. Those are your ad creative for Stage 3.

## Stage 2 (month 2): Raise average order value

**Trigger: steady 1–2 orders/day.**

- **Bundles.** Create "The Shedding Kit" (glove + brush + roller) at $44.99 — that's one Payment Link, ~$22 net profit per order. Add it to `products.js` as a featured product. Bundles are the cheapest path to $1k/month because they cut the orders you need almost in half.
- **Post-purchase upsell:** on the Stripe success page message, offer a second unit at 20% off through a dedicated discount Payment Link.
- **Email the list weekly:** one pet tip + one product. Even a small list converts at 2–5% per send.

## Stage 3 (month 2–3): Add paid traffic — carefully

**Trigger: one video format has proven it converts organically (views → link clicks → sales), AND you have $200+ of profit banked to reinvest.**

- Run your best-performing organic videos as **TikTok Spark Ads** at $10–20/day. Never run ads on untested creative.
- Math that must hold: cost per acquisition < net profit per order. At $10 net/order, a $10 CPA is break-even — bundles (Stage 2) are what make ads profitable, which is why ads come after bundles.
- Kill any ad set that spends 2× your target CPA without a sale. No exceptions, no "it's about to turn around."

## Stage 4 (month 3+): Automate fulfillment for real

**Trigger: 3+ orders/day, manual fulfillment eating >30 min/day.**

- Move to **AutoDS** (~$30–60/mo): auto-forwards orders to suppliers, syncs stock and tracking numbers automatically. At 3 orders/day it pays for itself in saved time; below that it's a subscription eating your margin — which is why it isn't in week 1.
- Alternatively, at consistent volume, CJ can hold **private-label stock** (your brand on packaging) — better margins, faster shipping, and it makes your store defensible against copycats.
- Consider migrating the storefront to Shopify **only now** — when a multi-item cart, reviews apps, and one-click upsells earn more than the $39/mo they cost. Your products, pricing, content library, and audience all transfer; the static site was the right tool for stage 0–3.

## Stage 5: The $1k/month steady state

At this point the machine looks like:

| Channel | Orders/month | Net |
|---|---|---|
| Organic short-form (60–90 videos/mo) | ~40 | ~$450 |
| Paid Spark Ads on proven creative | ~25 | ~$250 (after ad spend) |
| Email list + repeat buyers | ~15 | ~$200 |
| Marketplace listings (passive) | ~10 | ~$100 |

Time cost: ~1–1.5 hrs/day (content) + ~20 min/day (ops with AutoDS).

## Standing rules

1. **Never add a fixed cost before the trigger fires.** Every $30/mo subscription is 3 orders/month you owe before profit.
2. **Reinvest the first $500** (samples, domain, micro-creator seeding, first ad budget) — don't drain the engine early.
3. **Watch refund rate.** Above ~5%, fix the product or the delivery promise before scaling traffic — scaling a leaky bucket just buys more leaks.
4. **Set aside ~25% of profit for taxes.** You're running a business now; an LLC + separate bank account is worth doing once you clear ~$500/mo.
