## Week 1 video scripts (3/day × 7 days)

Film each as one continuous phone clip unless noted. Text-on-screen in quotes; say nothing on camera unless marked VOICE. Post times: ~7–9am, ~12–1pm, ~7–10pm (peak scroll hours).

### Day 1 — Grooming Glove
1. **The Peel.** Close-up: glove covered in fur after brushing → slowly peel the fur off in one sheet. Text: "the most satisfying part of owning a husky". Caption: "No more fur tumbleweeds 🧤 link in bio #petproducts #doghack #sheddingseason"
2. **Cat reaction.** Film a cat leaning into the glove mid-groom. Text: "she thinks it's petting. it's a lint trap." Caption: "Gentle enough that she asks for it #cattok #catproducts"
3. **Amount challenge.** Pile all fur brushed in one session next to the pet. Text: "all of this was ON the dog 10 minutes ago". Caption: "Where does it all come from?? #dogsoftiktok"

### Day 2 — Hair Remover Roller
4. **Before/after couch.** Split shot: fur-covered sofa cushion → roll → clean. Text: "I was about to throw this couch away". Caption: "Reusable, no refills ever #cleantok #petHack"
5. **The dump.** Open the roller chamber and dump the collected fur. Text: "one week of couch fur". Caption: "Oddly satisfying #satisfying #petparents"
6. **Car seats.** Roll the back seat of a car. Text: "dog tax: paid in fur". Caption: "Road trip recovery #dogmom #carcleaning"

### Day 3 — Slow Feeder
7. **The timer.** VOICE + timer on screen: dog finishes a normal bowl in ~10 seconds → same portion in the maze bowl takes 2+ minutes. Text: "9 seconds vs 2 minutes". Caption: "Vets recommend slow feeders for gulpers #doghealth #slowfeeder"
8. **The frustration (cute).** Dog nose-deep working the maze. Text: "he's mad but his stomach thanks him". Caption: "Better digestion, less bloat #puppytok"
9. **POV.** Text-only over the dog eating: "POV: you inhale dinner and immediately look for more". Caption: "Sound familiar? #dogsbelike"

### Day 4 — LED Collar
10. **Dark reveal.** Pitch-black yard → collar flicks on, dog visible instantly. Text: "night walks hit different now". Caption: "Visible from 500m #dogsafety #nightwalk"
11. **Distance shot.** Film from far down the street — the moving light is all you see. Text: "that's my dog, 200m away". Caption: "USB rechargeable, no batteries #dogdad"
12. **Modes.** Cycle steady → slow flash → fast flash. Text: "pick your vibe". Caption: "8hr battery, 15min charge #petgear"

### Day 5 — Snuffle Mat
13. **The hide.** Speed-up: scattering kibble into the fleece folds → dog demolishes it nose-first. Text: "15 min of sniffing = 1 hour of walking". Caption: "Trainers use these for anxious dogs #dogtraining #enrichment"
14. **Tired dog.** Dog passed out post-snuffle. Text: "snuffle mat: 1, zoomies: 0". Caption: "For high-energy pups #puppyenergy"
15. **Rainy day.** Rain on window → dog on mat inside. Text: "when it's too wet to walk". Caption: "Indoor enrichment #rainyday #dogmom"

### Day 6 — Self-Cleaning Brush
16. **The click.** Brush full of fur → press button → fur pad drops off. Text: "the button is everything". Caption: "Never pick hair out of a brush again #groomingtok"
17. **Undercoat.** Show the fine bent bristles reaching deep undercoat on a fluffy pet. Caption: "Rounded tips, zero scratching #catgrooming"
18. **Duo.** Glove for the body, brush for the fluff — quick two-step routine. Caption: "The shedding routine #petcare"

### Day 7 — Best-of + store
19. **Remix** your best-performing clip with a new hook line.
20. **Haul.** All six products on a table, 2s each. Text: "I started a pet store and these are the only 6 things we sell". Caption: "Tested picks only 🐾 link in bio"
21. **Founder clip.** You, on camera, 15s: why you started the store, the 30-day guarantee. People buy from people.

**After posting:** reply to every comment within an hour. If any video passes ~5k views, film 3 variations of it the next day instead of the schedule.
