# Cat Content Playbook — Scripts for YOUR Cat

You have a cat, so lean into #CatTok — it's one of the biggest, most engaged communities on the platform and it converts well for impulse pet buys. These scripts use products your cat can actually demo. Film vertical (9:16), phone only, natural light, 8–20 seconds each.

**The rule that matters most:** the first 1 second must stop the scroll, and the product must prove itself visually by second 2–3. If a viewer has to think, they scroll.

---

## Product priority for a cat (film in this order)

1. **Feather Wand Teaser** — cats go wild on camera; highest "watchable" factor
2. **Pet Grooming Mitt** — the "she thinks it's petting" angle + visible fur
3. **Self-Cleaning Slicker Brush** — fur removal is oddly satisfying content
4. **Snuffle Mat / Lick Mat** — enrichment angle, calm satisfying content
5. **Slow Feeder** — for cats that scarf food and vomit

## The 4 rules that decide whether this works

1. **Volume + consistency.** 2–3 posts/day for at least 2–3 weeks. Most videos flop; you're buying lottery tickets until one hits. One winner can do 100k+ views.
2. **Never repost supplier footage.** TikTok suppresses recycled clips. Always your own cat, your own phone.
3. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Comments feed the algorithm harder than likes.
4. **When one video pops (>5k views), immediately make 3 variations of it.** Ride the winner; don't move on.
