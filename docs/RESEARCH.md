# Market Research — Why This Store Is Built the Way It Is

_Research date: July 2026._

## Niche choice: pet accessories

Three criteria drove the pick, based on current (2026) dropshipping data:

1. **Reliability.** Pet care is consistently cited as one of the most reliable dropshipping niches — owners buy year-round, on impulse, and repeatedly.
2. **Organic-marketing fit.** With a $0 ad budget, your traffic comes from short-form video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts). The niches that work organically are the ones where a product proves its value visually in under two seconds — pet products top that list alongside kitchen gadgets and home organization.
3. **Margins.** The specific products in `products.js` (grooming glove, self-cleaning brush, hair remover roller) are among the highest-gross-margin dropshipping products tracked in 2026 — 85–95% gross margin before shipping and fees.

## Product selection

Every product in the catalog passes four filters:

| Filter | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Demos itself in <2s of video | Required for organic TikTok to work |
| Solves a felt problem (shedding, gulping, night safety, boredom) | Problem-solvers outsell novelties |
| Lightweight and unbreakable | Cheap shipping, low refund/damage rate |
| $14–$25 retail | Impulse-buy range; no purchase deliberation |

## Fulfillment: CJ Dropshipping (free plan)

Compared for 2026: **CJ Dropshipping** (free to join, 50+ global warehouses including US stock, huge catalog), **Zendrop** (easiest UX but real automation starts at $49/mo), **AutoDS** (best automation, paid, worth it at scale — see GROWTH.md).

Verdict for week 1: **CJ Dropshipping** — $0/month, US warehouse stock for 7–12 day delivery, and it fulfills orders you paste in manually. Switch to AutoDS when order volume justifies its subscription (see `GROWTH.md`).

## Checkout: Stripe Payment Links instead of Shopify

- Shopify: $39/mo + app fees. At a $100/week goal that's a meaningful chunk of your profit.
- Stripe Payment Links: **$0/month**, no code, just 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction. Hosted checkout page, Apple Pay/Google Pay included, collects the shipping address for you.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages or Vercel — free.

Total fixed cost to launch: **~$10 for a domain (optional in week 1)**. Everything else is per-sale cost.

## Reality check (read this)

Honest numbers from the organic-dropshipping guides, not the hype:

- Organic TikTok typically takes **30–60 days of consistent posting** (1–3 videos/day) before sales are steady. Some stores get sales in days when one video hits — but that's variance, not a plan.
- **$100 profit in week 1 is achievable but not guaranteed.** The playbook stacks the odds by combining organic video with faster channels (Facebook Marketplace, local groups, your own network) that don't need an audience.
- A "good" net margin after all costs is 20–30%. This store's unit economics are set up for roughly 50% net per order because there's no ad spend and no platform fees at the start.
