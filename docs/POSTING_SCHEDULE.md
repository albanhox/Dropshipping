# Week 1 Posting Calendar — Copy, Paste, Schedule

You chose the organic path. Here's the whole first week pre-built so it's dead simple:

**The one-time work (≈20-30 min):** film 12 short clips of your cat using the scripts in `CAT_CONTENT.md`. Do it in one sitting. Vertical (hold phone upright), good light, 8-20 sec each.

**Then (≈15 min):** load them into a scheduler and paste the captions below. The scheduler auto-posts all week — you're done.

**Schedulers (pick one, all free tier):**
- TikTok's built-in scheduler (desktop tiktok.com → upload → schedule)
- Meta Business Suite (auto-posts Instagram + Facebook Reels)
- Later / Metricool / Buffer (schedule all platforms at once)

Post 2/day: once **~8-9am**, once **~7-9pm** (peak scroll). Cross-post each to TikTok + Reels + Shorts.

## Rules that make or break it
1. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — comments boost reach more than likes.
2. **Store link in your bio from post #1.** Captions say "link in bio."
3. **If any video passes ~5k views, drop the schedule and make 3 variations of it that day.** Riding a winner is where the real views (and sales) come from.
4. **Don't quit at day 7.** Most videos flop; you're posting until one catches. 40+ posts is a fair test, not 12.

## After week 1
Refill this calendar with new clips. Keep whichever product/angle got the most views front-and-center. When one video clearly converts (clicks → sales), that's your green light for the paid-ads plan in `ADS.md`.
